# SheWellRX voice

Read `docs/voice-and-terminology.md` in this repository before writing. The rules that matter most:

1. **The patient is the subject of every sentence.** Never frame copy around the doctor ("the women he treats"). Use "you", "we", "your clinician", "a board certified ObGyn"; use "Dr. Copur" only where a credential, promise, or legal statement needs a name.
2. **Clinical and gentle.** Medical names first, one plain-word explanation after: "vasomotor symptoms (hot flashes and night sweats)", "genitourinary syndrome of menopause". No slang, no dramatized distress, no blunt negatives ("not a shrug", "not embarrassments"), no exclamation points, no "real" or "actually" as intensifiers.
3. **Honest.** "When clinically appropriate", "many women", "results vary". A video visit is a medical evaluation, never a guaranteed prescription.
4. **Respectful.** "women", "patients", "you". Never "ladies" or "girls".
5. **Structure.** Eyebrow = clinical category. Headline = the patient's outcome or question. Buttons = an action the patient takes ("Schedule a video visit"). Reviews = "Patient reviews" with Google attribution.

Terminology quick list: clinician (not "our doctors"), video visit, care plan, evaluation, perimenopause and menopause, menopausal hormone therapy (MHT), vaginal dryness / GSM, pain with intimacy, low sexual desire, heavy menstrual bleeding, irregular cycles, contraception (lists) / birth control (headings), self-pay practice with transparent pricing (not "cash only"), in-person evaluation, secure messaging, the women in our care.
