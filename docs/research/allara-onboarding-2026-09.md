# Allara Health as a reference model for SheWellRX onboarding

Research date: 2026-09-01. Sources are cited inline by URL. Anything not directly observed is marked **[unverified]**.

How the Allara questionnaire was captured: `https://onboarding.allarahealth.com/` is a Formsort flow (the HTML `<meta name="constants">` names client `Allara`, flow `onboardingv0`, host `onboarding.allarahealth.com`, and the JS bundles `/assets/flow/static/js/flow.*.js`). WebFetch returns an empty shell, so the page was opened in a browser tab and the flow definition the page itself downloads (`GET https://onboarding.allarahealth.com/flow-api/client/Allara/deployment/07ed5eaa-e6c1-4fde-b654-b95f5ca55ec2`, 178 KB, deployed 2026-08-31, variant label `wm13-control`) was read from the network log and parsed. The secondary `checkinsurance` flow (`https://onboarding.allarahealth.com/flow/checkinsurance`, variant `check-insurance-v9-control`, deployment `d273c1f5-...`) was captured the same way. No answers were submitted. Everything in section 2 is therefore the literal production flow definition as of 2026-08-31, not a reconstruction from reviews.

## 2. Allara's onboarding questionnaire, step by step (from the production flow definition)

### 2a. Global structure and chrome

- Platform: Formsort (`<meta name="powered_by" content="formsort">`), HIPAA security policy on (`securityPolicy.isHIPAA: true`), "Powered by" link hidden, Sentry, Amplitude (events UrlOpened, FlowLoaded, StepLoaded, StepCompleted, FlowFinalized, EmailCollected; `sendAnswers: true`; `PhoneCollected: false`) and GTM (`GTM-TNV8865`) integrations. Behavior flags: `enableUrlNavigation: true` (each step gets a URL, so `#1`-style hashes are not how it routes), `resumeSessionOnPageRefresh: true`, `returningResponderPlacement: StartAtBeginningKeepAnswers`.
- Layout (screenshot of first screen, 800 px wide): circular "A" logo top-center; a three-node progress bar labeled **Welcome · Eligibility · Create Account** (in the insurance-first variant: **Insurance · Eligibility · Create Account**); a small "< Back" text button; serif display headline; short grey subhead; full-width white answer buttons stacked vertically; page background `rgb(244,248,249)`; text column max-width 350 px; single column, mobile-first. Buttons: Inter 700, 16 px, radius 8 px, light-blue fill `rgb(90,137,255)` for the primary action; single-select answers `autoAdvance: true` (tapping an option advances without a Continue button).
- Three groups, 23 steps, most steps conditional. `what_brings_you_to_allara` (the very first answer) drives five parallel branches: `treatment_of_condition`, `manage_symptoms`, `weight_loss`, `fertility`, `understand_hormonal_health`.
- Flow variant in production on 2026-08-31 is labeled `wm13-control` (an A/B experiment; the answer keys also include `experiment`, `variant_label`, `vip`, utm_* and `form_includes_insurance_question`).

### 2b. Main flow `onboardingv0` (entered from "Get started" / "Continue →")

**GROUP 1 — Welcome (14 steps)**

1. `what_brings_you` — Headline "Every woman's journey is different, tell us about yours". Sub "We start where other providers stop. Let's begin with what matters most to you." Question (bold) "What brings you here today?" Single-select buttons, auto-advance: **Treat a known condition / Diagnose unexplained symptoms / Weight loss / Fertility support / Balance my hormones**.
2. `trustpilot_interstitial` — Social-proof interstitial: Trustpilot image (350 px) + "Don't just take our word for it / Women come to Allara looking for answers. Here's what they found." (No question.)
3. `what_brings_you_details` — one of five questions is shown, depending on step 1:
   - Condition branch: "What's the main condition you're looking to address? / Select one primary condition — we'll personalize your care from here." Single-select: **Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) / Hormonal Imbalance / Hypothyroidism/Hashimoto's / Metabolic syndrome/insulin resistance / Endometriosis / Perimenopause / Menopause / Other** (+ text "Please describe any other diagnosis" if Other).
   - Symptoms branch: "What are you experiencing? / Select all that apply – this helps us personalize your care." Multi-select: **Irregular periods / Unexplained weight changes / Acne / Hair loss / Excessive hair growth / Fatigue / Hot flashes / Anxiety / Mood changes / Something else** (+ text, 200-char max, "Please describe the symptoms you're experiencing").
   - Weight-loss branch: "What's making this feel like the right moment for a change?" Multi-select: My weight has changed or increased recently / It affects how I feel day to day / Something in my life changed / I've been meaning to for a while and finally have the time or support / What I've tried before hasn't worked / Something else (+ "Please explain").
   - Fertility branch: "Which best describes you?" Single-select: I want to understand my fertility / I have been trying to conceive for under 6 months / I have been trying to conceive for 6+ months.
   - Hormones branch: "Which best describes you?" Single-select: I'm curious if I have a hormonal imbalance / I feel well but I want to check my hormones.
4. `what_brings_you_follow_up` (has a completion animation) — again branch-specific:
   - Condition: "If you were formally diagnosed, how long ago was that?" Single-select: Less than a year ago / 1-5 years ago / 5+ years ago / I haven't received an official diagnosis.
   - Symptoms: header "Take a moment to reflect on how often you notice these symptoms / This helps us build a care plan that actually fits with what you're experiencing." then one **5-point slider per selected symptom** (labels "Rarely" … "Always"; choices 1–5 with the numbers visually hidden) for Irregular periods, Unexplained weight changes, Acne, Hair loss, Hair growth, Fatigue, Hot flashes, Anxiety, Mood changes, and the free-text "other" symptom.
   - Fertility (TTC): "What have you already tried?" Multi-select: Nothing yet / Ovulation tracking or at-home testing / Fertility specialist visits / IVF / IUI / Something else. Fertility (planning): "What have you done to evaluate your own fertility?" Multi-select: Tracking cycles and ovulation / Lab testing (AMH, FSH, estradiol) / Considering egg freezing / Egg freezing / Genetic testing / Something else.
   - Hormones: "Why check in on your hormones now?" Multi-select: I've noticed changes in my body, mood, or cycle recently / A doctor or provider suggested it / Something in my life changed (family planning, a friend's diagnosis, etc.) / I just like to stay on top of my health proactively / Something else.
   - Weight loss: "What's your current height, weight, and goal weight? / We'll see what's right for your body as we work together, but it's helpful to know how much you think you'd like to lose." Section labels "WHERE YOU ARE TODAY" (Height (feet), Height (inches), Current weight (lbs) — numeric inputs) and "WHERE YOU'D LIKE TO BE" (Goal weight (lbs)).
5. `what_brings_you_second_follow_up` (skipped for fertility) — "what have you tried" by branch:
   - Condition: "What have you tried already? Select all that apply." Hormone medication (birth control, HRT) / Thyroid medication / Supplements / Lifestyle changes / Weight loss medications / GLP-1s / Something else / Nothing yet.
   - Symptoms: "What made you decide to look into this now?" My symptoms have gotten worse recently / I've been putting this off and finally have the time to get support / What I've been trying hasn't been working / Something in my life changed (planning a pregnancy, a new diagnosis, a life event) / Something else.
   - Weight loss: "Which weight loss methods have you tried? Select all that apply – this helps us personalize your care." Lifestyle changes / Intermittent fasting / Calorie restriction / Counting macros / Keto or other specialized diets / Weight loss medications / GLP-1s / Something else.
   - Hormones: "What have you tried so far?" Medications / Supplements / Lifestyle changes / Diet & nutrition / Tracking symptoms / At-home hormone tests / Something else / Nothing yet.
6. `condition_interstitial` — personalized reassurance screen: a branch-specific illustration (250 px), eyebrow "TRUSTED BY OVER 100,000 WOMEN", and one of: "*Finally*, a team that understands PCOS / Your Allara care team will connect your symptoms, labs, and nutrition — then will develop a treatment plan built around you." · "\"Normal\" thyroid labs. Still feel off? / Symptoms can linger even when thyroid labs are "normal." We look beyond the numbers to guide your care." · "Your symptoms help guide your care…" (hormonal imbalance) · "Care for all your Endometriosis symptoms / From anti-inflammatory nutrition to pain management strategies…" · "Your symptoms deserve answers / Your Allara care team will identify if perimenopause is driving your symptoms…" · "Menopause is *more than hot flashes*…" · "It's not about your *"willpower"*…" (metabolic) · "We're where you go when you *don't know where to go*…" (Other) · "*Finally* understand what's driving your symptoms…" (symptoms branch) · "98% of patients are satisfied with their results / Our care team address the root causes of weight gain — hormones, insulin resistance, and metabolism — not just calories in, calories out." (weight) · "Your hormonal health deserves *an expert care team*…" (hormones) · "Fertility planning is *more than timing*…" (fertility).
7. `timeline_interstitial` — "A care experience *you can look forward to* / From your first visit to feeling your best — here's what's ahead." + a 350 px timeline image.
8. `sex_at_birth` — "What sex were you assigned at birth?" Single-select: Female / Male / Intersex.
9. `no_eligible_sex` (read-only, forward navigation disabled, back allowed) — shown only when `sex_at_birth == male`: "Unfortunately, you're not eligible for our care program at this time. / Based on your gender at birth, you are not medically eligible for our program at this time. If you feel this was an error, feel free to go back and edit your response." (Note: the live logic gates only `male`; a review claiming Intersex is also disqualified, https://glp-1.reviews/allara-health/, does not match the current definition.)
10. `is_pregnant` (skipped on the fertility branch) — "Are you pregnant?" Single-select: No / No, but I'm trying to conceive / Yes.
11. `no_eligible_ttc` (read-only dead end) — when Yes: "Unfortunately, you're not eligible for our care program at this time. / While Allara can help with fertility, our program is not a good fit for women who are already pregnant. If you feel this was an error, feel free to go back and adjust your pregnancy status."
12. `service_eligibility` — "What's your date of birth?" Three dropdowns (M-D-Y, month as "MMM", past-only, flagged `isDateOfBirth`). Computed answer keys `age`, `age_eligible`, `age_64`.
13. `no_eligible_age` (read-only dead end) — when `age < 18` or `age > 64`: "Unfortunately, you're not eligible for our care program at this time. / **Allara is designed for women between 18 and 64.** It looks like you entered a birth date that does not fit our care program requirements. If you feel this was an error, feel free to go back and adjust your date of birth."
14. `eligible_candidate` — positive-reinforcement bridge with a photo: "You seem like a great candidate for our program! / Now let's do a quick insurance check to see if you're covered. We work with most major plans and offer affordable self-pay options if your insurance doesn't cover care."

**GROUP 2 — Eligibility (8 steps; really the insurance/payment-path decision)**

1. `using_insurance` — "Do you plan to use insurance?" Single-select: Yes / No, I'm paying out-of-pocket.
2. `zip_code` (savepoint) — "What is your zip code?" postal-code input + info-icon microcopy "We'll need this information to match you to your care team" + Continue. Behind the scenes the zip resolves `state_field`, `is_eligible_join` (state served) and `ehr_eligibility` via Allara's API (`allara_api_base_url`, `api_secret` answer keys).
3. `insurance_eligibility` (savepoint; only if eligible state AND using insurance) — "Who is your health insurance provider?" Single-select whose choices are loaded live from Allara's API (`choicesUrl` + `x-formsort-secret` header); the mapper relabels "United Healthcare" as "United Healthcare or UMR" and appends "I have Medicare or Medicaid" (+ text "Please enter your insurance provider" if Other). Sets `is_insurance_eligible`, `insurance_plan` (e.g. "COMMERCE"), `is_insurance_for_show_only`.
4. (unnamed, when `is_eligible_join == false`) — "Unfortunately you're not eligible for our care program at this time. / We don't currently operate in the state you entered, or we don't recognize your zip code. If you think you reached this page in error, please go back and try again."
5. `insurance_coverage` (in-network) — "Great news! We take your insurance / You'll only pay your standard copay and deductible for your visits." + product image + Continue.
6. `self_pay_out_of_pocket` — "Let's get your care started / Start care for ~~$149*~~ **$119** for your first month. HSA/FSA eligible." Product image; three check-bullets "1:1 virtual visits with your medical provider and registered dietitian", "Access to comprehensive hormonal & metabolic testing", "Personalized care plans, with clinicians reviewing every result"; button text "Continue personalizing my program"; footnote "$149 billed monthly. Cancel anytime."
7. `self_pay_medicare_or_medicaid` — same layout, headline "Start with your care team today / Start care for ~~$149~~ **$119** for your first month."
8. `self_pay_insurance_not_accepted` (out-of-network commercial plan) — "We'll personalize your care from here / While we don't accept your plan at the moment, you can start care for ~~$149~~ **$119** for your first month. HSA/FSA eligible." Same bullets/footnote/button.

**GROUP 3 — Create Account (1 step)**

1. `email_capture` — "Now let's get you set up / We'll use this to create your secure space so you can book your first appointment". Fields: First Name, Last Name, Email (typo auto-correct on). Lock-icon microcopy "Your information is 100% secure, HIPAA compliant and will not be shared." One required checkbox: "By checking this box, I agree to receive communications and to Allara's [Terms of Use](https://allarahealth.com/terms-conditions/) and [Privacy Policy](https://allarahealth.com/privacy-policy/)". Continue button. Then, below the fold and optional: "How did you hear about us? (optional)" dropdown (Event or webinar, Facebook, Friend or family, Google, Health app or device, Influencer, Instagram, Medical provider, My employer, My insurance, Podcast, Reddit, TikTok, Wellness business, Other) with conditional follow-ups (a 33-name influencer list, a 6-show podcast list, provider/employer/app/wellness-business free text, a webinar list including "GLP1 Webinar", "Monday Girl Summit 2026", "Your Cycle Decoded Webinar").
   - On completion the flow redirects to `signup_url` returned by Allara's API, falling back to `https://app.allarahealth.com/signup` (a JS app; not renderable by WebFetch).

**What is NOT in the pre-signup questionnaire:** phone number, password, address, medication list, allergies, medical history, safety/contraindication screening, payment card, scheduling. Those happen in `app.allarahealth.com` after account creation. Independent accounts put the order there as: choose payment path (insurance vs "$149/month" self-pay) → schedule a 30-minute provider visit (browse providers, next-day slots) → text+email confirmation → clinical intake (DOB, address, emergency contact, family history, medications/supplements) → later a "three-minute questionnaire" before the dietitian visit (https://www.thequalityedit.com/articles/allara-health-review). One review counts "6 distinct stages … approximately 4 minutes across 18 screens" ending in "Program Selection & Checkout" (https://glp-1.reviews/allara-health/) **[screen count unverified; consistent with the definition above]**. Whether the card is charged at checkout or only after the first visit is **[unverified]**; Allara's Services page says a card "is required to be kept on file for subscription fees to be collected monthly" (https://www.allarahealth.com/services).

### 2c. Secondary flow `checkinsurance` (entered from every "Check Insurance" button)

Same three-node progress bar, relabeled **Insurance · Eligibility · Create Account**. Order is inverted so that the insurance answer comes first:

1. "Let's start with your zip code" + info box "We use this to confirm your insurance works with Allara." + Continue.
2. "Who is your health insurance provider?" (API-loaded list, "I have Medicare or Medicaid", Other → text).
3. Outcome screen: "Great news! We take your insurance…" / Medicare-Medicaid self-pay offer / "We'll personalize your care from here / While we don't accept your plan at the moment, you can start care for ~~$149~~ **$119**…" (all with "Continue personalizing my program").

## 3. UX patterns that make the Allara flow convert (and the evidence)

1. **One decision per screen, auto-advance.** Every single-select has `autoAdvance: true`; the only screens with a Continue button are text/number inputs and interstitials. Average path is roughly 12–15 screens for a condition-branch user (definition in 2b).
2. **Named-phase progress bar, not a percentage.** Three labeled nodes (Welcome / Eligibility / Create Account) set expectations and make the whole thing look short; the Formsort setting `showAllSteps: false` hides the true step count.
3. **Answer-driven personalization within the flow.** The first answer fans out into five branches; the reassurance interstitial (step 6) reflects the exact condition back ("Finally, a team that understands PCOS", "'Normal' thyroid labs. Still feel off?"). Answer keys like `program`, `experiment`, `variant_label` show the flow is A/B tested (`wm13-control`).
4. **Social-proof interstitials placed at emotional low points**: a Trustpilot screen right after the first commitment (step 2), the "trusted by 100,000 women" reassurance after the symptom questions (step 6), a "what's ahead" timeline before the eligibility gates (step 7), and "You seem like a great candidate" immediately after the DOB gate (step 14).
5. **Symptom severity sliders** ("Rarely … Always") turn a checklist into reflection ("Take a moment to reflect on how often you notice these symptoms"), raising perceived personalization and sunk cost before the ask.
6. **Goal capture** (goal weight; "why now") makes the visitor articulate motivation before seeing a price.
7. **"Why we ask" microcopy** on sensitive fields: "We'll need this information to match you to your care team" (zip), "We use this to confirm your insurance works with Allara", "Pregnancy" and DOB gates explain the rule in the dead-end copy.
8. **Trust microcopy at the ask**: lock icon + "Your information is 100% secure, HIPAA compliant and will not be shared." on the email step.
9. **Price anchoring and softening**: strikethrough $149 → $119 first month, "HSA/FSA eligible", "$149 billed monthly. Cancel anytime.", and the button relabeled "Continue personalizing my program" rather than "Pay".
10. **Insurance as a hook, self-pay as the catch-all**: every insurance outcome (in-network, Medicare/Medicaid, out-of-network) has a positive next screen; no one is turned away for money.
11. **Late, optional attribution**: "How did you hear about us?" appears after the required fields, marked optional, so it never blocks conversion but still feeds marketing.
12. **Session persistence** (`resumeSessionOnPageRefresh`, savepoints at zip and insurance) and per-step URLs so a drop-off can return.
13. **Mobile-first single column**, 350 px text column, large tappable buttons, native dropdowns on mobile (`showNativeDropdownOnMobile: true`).
14. **Landing-page reinforcement**: the SEM landing page (https://www.allarahealth.com/get-ready-to-feel-like-your-best-self) repeats "Trusted by 100,000 women", "88% … feel better within 1 month" and has one CTA ("Continue →") into the flow.
