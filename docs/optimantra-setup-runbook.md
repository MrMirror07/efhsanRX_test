Written 2026-09-01 from a live walk through of the practice's OptiMantra admin (org "FIRSTCHOICE OB GYN GROUP LLC") plus OptiMantra's help center (https://optimantra.zendesk.com/hc/en-us). ## How the patient flow works end to end

1. shewellrx.com `/book` (guided start, runs in the browser, stores nothing): concern, two fit questions, goals, safety check, pregnancy, New Jersey location, age, visit type, price and Good Faith Estimate notice.
2. Handoff to OptiMantra with the service preselected (deep link lands on "Select a Date and Time"):
   - Initial Telehealth Visit: `https://www.optimantra.com/optimus/patient/patientaccess/practsNslotsNEW?sid=dnFWenUvMC9rVGt3bG40cGlSRCtqUT09&pid=bXNNTmxZTG9lMSt3RXlUbmpyaW1HQT09&lid=d0lHZEVzR0JTeG5ucWgya2RRNm93QT09`

   - Full menu (both services): `https://www.optimantra.com/optimus/patient/patientaccess/servicesall?pid=bXNNTmxZTG9lMSt3RXlUbmpyaW1HQT09&lid=d0lHZEVzR0JTeG5ucWgya2RRNm93QT09`
3. OptiMantra step 2: pick a time (Eastern). Step 3 "Complete Booking": demographics (fields controlled by Communications > Patient Portal and Kiosk > Demographic Profile), consent, and payment. Location Settings already has **Payment For Booking = Full Payment Is Required**, so the card is charged at the end of booking (DONE).
4. OptiMantra redirects to `https://shewellrx.com/booked` (Confirmation page, DONE) and emails the confirmation with the portal link.
5. The portal shows the forms tied to the booked service: consent "SheWellRX Telehealth Consent and Terms of Care" (tied to both services, DONE) and questionnaire "GENERAL_Intake & History" for Initial / "GENERAL_*Follow-Up" for follow ups (DONE, to be replaced by the SheWellRX questionnaires in `docs/intake-questionnaires.md`).
6. Reminder email/text carries `VIDEO_CHAT_LINK`; the patient joins the built in Twilio video lobby without logging in. Dr. Copur starts the call from the appointment's Actions menu > Video Chat.
7. After the visit: chart note, e prescription (eRx add on is active), superbill/receipt, portal messaging for 14 days.

Patient portal sign in link (used on the site): `https://www.optimantra.com/optimus/om/patient/login?accessPoint=L1YvN0JQYzk1ZnRsdlFXNEhOOXJWUT09`

## What OptiMantra cannot do (design around it)

- No public API, no inbound webhooks, no Zapier app. Outbound webhooks exist (Settings > Marketing > Other CRM Integration: triggers Booking, Cancellation, Check-out, Superbill, up to 20 mapped fields) if a CRM is ever added. CRM "connector links" can prefill demographics in the booking URL (`first`, `last`, `email`, `ph`, `dob`, `address`, `city`, `state`, `zip`, `src`); the site deliberately does not use these (no personal data in URLs).
- Booking step 3 has an optional "Reason For Visit Or Comments" box, which is where the patient can paste the summary the site offers to copy. The scheduler can be framed (no X-Frame-Options; `&hideHeaderInFrame=1&hideFooterInFrame=1` hides the practice header and OptiMantra footer), but its session cookie is SameSite=Lax, so an embedded multi step booking must be tested before relying on it; the site links out in the same tab instead, which OptiMantra recommends.
- Booking creates the patient record but not a portal login; the confirmation email must tell the patient to use "New Sign Up" with the same email, first name, and date of birth. Whether online booked patients get "Access to Patient Portal" automatically must be confirmed on the first test booking.
- Video: Twilio, browser only, no recording, 1:1 calls up to 3 hours; only the practitioner who owns the appointment should send the video link (a staff sent link creates a mismatched room).
- OptiMantra merged with Cerbo in December 2025; the products still run separately. Keep exports (first one is free with 4 weeks notice) if the roadmap changes.
- Questionnaires have no conditional logic; sections carry "skip if not applicable" instructions instead, and one questionnaire is tied per service.
- Service Type has no "Telehealth" value; use Place of Service 10 and modifier 95 on codes.
- One deposit amount per location; full payment is the right setting for flat fees.
- The scheduler cannot be embedded with a script; link or iframe only. The site links.
